# Dataset

**Location:** Anand Vihar, Delhi

**Period:** January 2025 to August 2026

**Frequency:** Hourly

**Size:** 14,592 observations and 11 pollutants

Training and validation cover 2025; testing covers 2026. Missing measurements remain blank in the source data. The source CSV is excluded from this repository; its filename and checksum are recorded in `manifest.json`.
